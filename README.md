# Grok Custom Skills

Personal collection of custom skills used with Grok for legal research, contract analysis, document processing, translation, compliance (EU/Polish law, NIS2, sanctions), and productivity tools.

These skills are tailored for in-house counsel work involving Chinese-listed company operations, Polish subsidiary management, battery materials supply chain, EU regulations (PPWR, NZIA, TCA, IAA, etc.), and academic translation workflows.

## Skills Included

- commercial-legal-pl: Polish commercial law contract analysis and drafting (KTZR style)
- conference-paragraph-transcript: Conference audio to speaker-separated paragraph transcripts
- contract-review: Contract review and risk analysis
- docx-processing-superdoc: Word document processing with SuperDoc CLI
- eu-pl-law-tracker: EU and Polish law tracking and analysis
- foreign-law-research: Foreign law research (non-China jurisdictions)
- full-document-no-summary: Enforce full unabbreviated document generation
- gzh-design: WeChat public account article layout engine
- lecture-video-bilingual-docx: Lecture/video transcripts with bilingual support and slides
- nis2-navigator: NIS2 / BSIG cybersecurity compliance navigator
- noisy-conference-asr-recovery: Recovery of unreadable audio stretches in transcripts
- pdf: PDF reading, creation, and transformation
- regulatory-deal-card-generator-patrick-munro: Interactive regulatory deal cards
- sanctions-screening-legal-analysis-skill-english-gillan-saleh: Sanctions and export control screening
- skill-optimizer-lawvable: Skill self-improvement optimizer
- translate-book: Full book translation pipeline (PDF/DOCX/EPUB to DOCX)
- zsxq-upload-strict: Strict rules for 知识星球 (ZSXQ) uploads

## Usage

These are Grok skills (typically placed in server-skills or equivalent). Each contains a SKILL.md with trigger conditions and instructions.

Generated and uploaded from the current Grok environment.
